About Lunaire Country Club

Our Mission

Lunaire Country Club provides California Bar exam preparation for the discerning space privateer. Our 18-hole lunar course offers an unparalleled study experience with Earth-views from every tee box.

Study Features

  • MBE-style multiple choice questions
  • AI-powered explanations and feedback
  • Performance tracking by subject and subtopic
  • Essay writing and grading modules

Club History

Founded in 1969 during the historic Apollo 11 mission, Lunaire Country Club has served generations of scholarly privateers seeking legal certification for their interstellar ventures. The Club maintains strict standards of excellence and welcomes members who appreciate the finer points of jurisprudence and low-gravity putting.

Membership in Lunaire Country Club implies acceptance of all Club bylaws, including provisions regarding the preservation of traditional lunar demographics. The Club's Admissions Committee meets quarterly to review applications and ensure alignment with our founding principles. Proper attire required at all times.
